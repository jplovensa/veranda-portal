DESIGN BASISVERANDA / UNIT 3BR

A retreat to share.
Space to call your own.

Ivory, walnut & quiet contrast.

Inspired by Veranda’s garden-and-resort setting, the shared rooms bring people together while three bedrooms offer privacy. Light gray marble in the shared living areas, wood-look SPC in the bedrooms, ivory fabrics, walnut joinery and quiet bronze details give the apartment its calm character. Finish direction remains subject to the existing IDR 225 million VAT-inclusive cap and supplier quotation.

The building stays in place.

Bedrooms, wet areas, service spine, entrance and lift context follow the uploaded drawing. Existing bathrooms remain untouched. The green EPS screen is an optional, unpriced dry-area concept subject to measured design and building approval.

Dimensions with a clear basis.

Only printed drawing datums are labelled as dimensions. Recesses, wall thickness, window geometry and furniture sizes are traced or assumed; ceiling height is assumed at 2.7 m. The source is a concept plan, not a site survey. No floor area is inferred from its chains.

A complete furnishing allowance.

IDR 225 million including VAT: IDR 215 million planned furnishing plus IDR 10 million controlled reserve. The scope prioritises useful storage, comfortable beds and shared living, with no bathroom work.

The setting: The Veranda Resort Residence, Lebak Bulus, South Jakarta. The developer’s resort concept brings gardens, outdoor leisure and apartment living together. Our furnishing direction carries that sense of retreat indoors. Explore Veranda’s setting ↗

Furnish the home. Retain the building.

Selected joinery and ready-stock pieces carry the design. Existing bathrooms, kitchen service points and the apartment structure remain in place. The optional green EPS screen requires its own measured quote, funded through approved offsets or reserve within the IDR 225 million cap.

Scope, exclusions & delivery

Includes the provisional items listed above, design coordination, delivery, installation and styling. An indicative six to eight weeks follows final scope, deposit and access confirmation; stock and fabrication govern the final programme.

Excludes bathroom work, demolition, structural changes, full kitchen replacement, concealed repairs, service relocation and new circuits. Building deposits and exceptional access charges are excluded. Air conditioners, cooker/hood, washing machine and water heater require an approved variation if needed.

Proposed payment stages

Based on the IDR 215 million planned package including VAT: 50% / IDR 107.5 million after final offer and selection approval; 30% / IDR 64.5 million before delivery after procurement confirmation; 20% / IDR 43 million at handover after agreed snag reconciliation. The IDR 10 million reserve is separate and requires itemised written approval. Final terms follow the confirmed offer.
