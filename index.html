01 / THE CONCEPTCOMPOSED FOR REAL LIFE

Classic character.
Everyday ease.

A warm, coherent home that feels settled from the moment you arrive.

Ivory opens up the rooms. Dark walnut gives the furniture definition. Restrained mouldings and bronze details bring a classic rhythm, balanced by simple silhouettes and soft, tactile textiles.

The furnishing package is intended for owner occupation or rental: useful storage, comfortable seating, a place to work and a consistent finish palette throughout.

FOR THE BUYER

A home that holds together.

Coordinated rooms, practical storage and selected components that can be repaired or replaced as needs change.

FOR THE RENTER

Room to settle in.

Spaces for rest, work and shared meals, with finishes selected for everyday care and routine turnover.

FOR THE PROPERTY

A consistent presentation.

A considered setting for viewings and listing photography. Rental returns and occupancy are not assumed.

PROPOSED SYSTEM / ILLUSTRATIVE PRODUCT VIEW
OPTIONAL / MODULAR EPS PARTITION

Designed with
change in mind.

A proposed dry-area privacy screen using EPS-core composite modules, protective board facings and accessible mechanical connections.

The design intent is longer use: replaceable finishes, planned disassembly and potential reuse. The complete supplier-designed assembly determines performance and detachability.

Fit, performance & cost

Veranda compatibility requires measured clearances, fixing locations, stability and building approval. Fire and acoustic evidence must be confirmed for the complete assembly. No certified environmental claim is made.

The partition is optional and unpriced. Its measured quotation must be funded through approved offsets or release of the existing reserve, within the IDR 190 million cap.

What the furnishing package includes

Measurement and design coordination; selected joinery and furniture; mattresses, curtains, lighting and decor; dry-area flooring, painting and minor existing-point connections; delivery, installation and styling. Appliance allowances cover one refrigerator, one TV and one microwave.

What stays outside the scope

The entire Veranda-provided bathroom remains untouched. Demolition, structural alterations, full kitchen replacement, concealed repairs, waterproofing, new circuits and service relocation are excluded. Building deposits and exceptional access charges are excluded. Air conditioners, cooker/hood, washing machine and water heater require a separately approved variation if needed.

Proposed payment stages & handover

Based on the IDR 180 million planned package including VAT: 50% / IDR 90 million after final offer and selection approval; 30% / IDR 54 million before delivery after procurement confirmation; 20% / IDR 36 million at handover after agreed snag reconciliation. Final terms follow the confirmed offer.

Proposed workmanship cover is 12 months from handover, subject to the final agreement. Manufacturer warranties remain separate.
