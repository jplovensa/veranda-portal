01 / THE CONCEPTTHE VERANDA RESORT RESIDENCE / LEBAK BULUS

A city address.
A resort state of mind.

Carry the calm of the gardens through your front door.

The Veranda’s resort setting is the starting point. Warm ivory keeps the rooms light; dark walnut gives them depth. Woven fabrics, quiet bronze details and restrained classic lines bring a sense of permanence without crowding the space.

The layout makes space for everyday rituals: coffee near the balcony, a meal together, a quiet bedroom and a place to work. Useful storage and replaceable loose furniture make the concept practical for both owners and renters.

The setting: The Veranda Resort Residence, Lebak Bulus, South Jakarta. The developer’s resort concept brings gardens, outdoor leisure and apartment living together. Our furnishing direction carries that sense of retreat indoors. Explore Veranda’s setting ↗

FOR THE BUYER

A retreat for everyday life.

A coordinated home with comfortable seating, useful storage and bedrooms that feel restful from the first night.

FOR THE RENTER

Easy to arrive. Easy to live.

A furnished setting for rest, work and meals, with straightforward care and selected pieces that can be replaced between tenancies.

FOR THE PROPERTY

A clear identity for the apartment.

A warm, recognisable interior for viewings and listing photography, connected to Veranda’s resort character. No rental yield or occupancy is promised.

06 / DELIVERY & SCOPEFROM CONCEPT TO HANDOVER

A clear path
to moving in.

An indicative six to eight weeks after scope approval, deposit and confirmed site access. Stock, fabrication and building delivery windows determine the final dates.

01 / WEEK 1

Measure.

Verify the unit, existing conditions, room dimensions and building requirements.

02 / WEEKS 1–2

Resolve.

Agree the measured plan, furniture models, samples and consolidated final offer.

03 / WEEKS 2–5

Make & source.

Procure approved products and release fabrication after stock and specification confirmation.

04 / WEEKS 5–8

Bring it home.

Deliver, install and style. Complete a joint inspection, snagging and documented handover.

What the furnishing package includes

Measurement and design coordination; selected joinery and furniture; mattresses, curtains, lighting and decor; dry-area flooring, painting and minor existing-point connections; delivery, installation and styling. Appliance allowances cover one refrigerator, one TV and one microwave.

What stays outside the scope

The entire Veranda-provided bathroom remains untouched. Demolition, structural alterations, full kitchen replacement, concealed repairs, waterproofing, new circuits and service relocation are excluded. Building deposits and exceptional access charges are excluded. Air conditioners, cooker/hood, washing machine and water heater require a separately approved variation if needed.

Proposed payment stages & handover

Based on the IDR 165 million planned package including VAT: 50% / IDR 82.5 million after final offer and selection approval; 30% / IDR 49.5 million before delivery after procurement confirmation; 20% / IDR 33 million at handover after agreed snag reconciliation. The IDR 10 million reserve is separate and requires itemised written approval. Final terms follow the confirmed offer.

Proposed workmanship cover is 12 months from handover, subject to the final agreement. Manufacturer warranties remain separate.
